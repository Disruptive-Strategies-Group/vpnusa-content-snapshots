2. Scope of This Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to personal information collected through the VPN USA website, including information submitted through:

  • Training registration and application forms
  • Job and internship application forms
  • Newsletter subscription forms
  • Other website forms that link to this Privacy Policy

It also applies to information you send to us by email after finding our contact information on the website.

VPN USA does not collect client intake information, case records, or referrals through website forms.

 

3. Information We Collect

Training Registrations and Applications

When you register for or apply to participate in a training, we may collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Organization
  • Professional role
  • City, state, or location
  • Training interests
  • Responses to application questions
  • Other information you choose to provide

Job and Internship Applications

When you apply for a job or internship, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact information
  • Résumé and cover letter
  • Education and employment history
  • Qualifications and professional experience
  • References
  • Work-authorization information
  • Other application materials you choose to submit

Please do not include sensitive personal information in an application unless it is relevant and specifically requested.

Newsletter Subscriptions

When you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and any other information requested on the subscription form.

You may unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe link in a newsletter or by contacting us.

Email Communications

Our website provides email addresses that visitors may use to contact VPN USA.

When you send us an email, we receive your email address and the information contained in your message, together with any attachments or other information you choose to provide. We use that information to review and respond to your inquiry.

VPN USA does not collect client intake information or case records through the website. Please do not send highly sensitive case information, confidential records, or emergency reports through ordinary email unless a VPN USA staff member has specifically requested the information and provided an appropriate communication method.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit the website, NationBuilder and other service providers may automatically collect limited technical and usage information, such as:

  • Internet Protocol address
  • Browser and device type
  • Operating system
  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access
  • Referring website
  • General location derived from an Internet Protocol address
  • Information about how visitors interact with the website

This information may be collected through cookies and similar technologies.

 

4. NationBuilder

Our website is hosted and managed through NationBuilder, a website, communications, and constituent-management platform operated by 3dna Corp.

Information submitted through our website forms may be stored and processed through NationBuilder on our behalf. NationBuilder may also collect technical and usage information when visitors interact with the website.

NationBuilder processes information according to its own contractual obligations and privacy practices. Its privacy policy is available on the NationBuilder website.

VPN USA determines how information submitted through our website is used for VPN USA’s organizational purposes.

8. Email and Newsletter Choices

You may unsubscribe from newsletters and promotional communications by using the unsubscribe link included in the message.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected.

For example:

  • Training records may be retained for administration, reporting, grant, or program-evaluation purposes.
  • Job and internship applications may be retained for the recruitment process and for a reasonable period afterward.
  • Newsletter information is retained until you unsubscribe or we no longer need it.
  • Email correspondence may be retained as needed to respond to inquiries and maintain appropriate organizational records.

We may retain information for a longer period when required for legal, tax, grant, contractual, insurance, employment, or security purposes.

 

10. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information.

However, no website, email system, or online storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information through website forms or ordinary email.
